More than a volume slider

The controls you want, where you need them.

Built for day-to-day listening, with controls and options for different sites and listening habits.

↗

Boost quiet media

Adjust site audio from −32 dB to +32 dB where the browser allows Web Audio routing. Positive boosts can distort loud material.

≋

Optional normalization

Automatically even out loud and quiet passages with Music and Adaptive modes, plus gain and peak-level feedback.

◖))

Mute & mono

Mute a site or combine stereo channels into mono for your preferred listening setup.

⌁

Remember per site

Keep volume and playback preferences for sites or URL paths when Remember is enabled.

⏻

Site-level controls

Toggle the extension on or off for the current site, and manage exclusions for domains or matching paths.

⚙

Fine-tune in Options

Configure normalization behavior, site exclusions, and other preferences from the full settings page.
